# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-26

First release.

- Clans with tags, names, invites (clickable accept/deny), open clans and `/clan join`
- Ranks: leader, officer, member — kick/promote/demote/transfer/disband with confirmations
- Clan chat and ally chat, as one-off messages or a toggle; clan tag in public chat
- Friendly-fire protection for clanmates and (optionally) allies, per-clan toggle
- Alliances with request/accept flow and a configurable limit
- Clan home with warmup, move-cancel and cooldown
- Kill/death stats, `/clan top`, `/clan list`, `/clan info`
- Chest menu (`/clan`) with member heads and one-click actions
- PlaceholderAPI placeholders including a leaderboard; optional Vault creation cost
- English and Traditional Chinese messages (MiniMessage)
- Paper 1.21.4 – 26.x and Folia
